      The paradox: a treatment that prevents death but does not cure (insulin, ART) raises prevalence. Remember P = I × D: anything that adds new cases or lengthens the time people live with the disease increases prevalence.
      Based on Bonita R, Beaglehole R, Kjellström T. Basic Epidemiology, 2nd ed. WHO; 2006 and Park's Textbook of PSM, 27th ed. (2023).
